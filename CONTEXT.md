# CamiloMartinezM.github.io

The personal academic website of Camilo Martínez: a short bio, publications and projects.

## Language

**Publication**:
A citable work with a bibliographic entry: a journal article, a conference paper or a thesis.
_Avoid_: paper (a thesis is a Publication too), research

**Selected publication**:
A Publication that is also featured on the home page.

**Project**:
A piece of work shown on the projects page. It may have its own page or only link elsewhere, and it may relate to Publications without being one.
_Avoid_: research

**News item**:
A short, dated announcement on the home page, such as a new Publication.
_Avoid_: announcement, update
